ABOUT KGOTHATSO FOUNDATION

Creating change where it matters.

We exist to support vulnerable children, empower young people and strengthen families and communities.

WHO WE ARE

People first. Always.

Kgothatso Foundation is a community-focused organisation committed to improving the lives of vulnerable children, youth and families.

We believe every person deserves dignity, opportunity and the support necessary to build a better future.

Our work focuses on practical interventions that respond to real needs within communities.

WHAT DRIVES US

A clear purpose. A lasting impact.

Everything we do is guided by our commitment to dignity, opportunity and stronger communities.

01

Our Mission

To improve the lives of vulnerable children, youth and families through education, support, empowerment and community-based programmes.

02

Our Vision

A future where every child and young person has access to the safety, education, resources and opportunities needed to reach their potential.

03

Our Approach

We work alongside communities rather than simply working for them. By listening to local needs and building partnerships, we create practical solutions.

OUR VALUES

The principles behind our work.

01 Dignity — treating every person with respect and compassion.
02 Integrity — acting responsibly, honestly and transparently.
03 Empowerment — creating opportunities for people to grow.
04 Community — building solutions together.
