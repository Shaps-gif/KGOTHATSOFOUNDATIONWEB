OUR PROGRAMMES

Practical action. Real opportunity.

Our programmes address immediate needs while creating pathways towards stronger and more resilient communities.

WHAT WE DO

Four programmes. One mission.

Each programme is designed around the needs of the communities we serve.

01

Child Support & Protection

We provide practical support to vulnerable children and families, helping connect people with essential resources and assistance.

  • Child support
  • Family assistance
  • Essential supplies
  • Community support
02

Education & Academic Support

Education can create opportunities that extend far beyond the classroom. Our initiatives support learning, digital skills and personal development.

  • Academic support
  • Digital literacy
  • Mentorship
  • Leadership development
03

Youth Empowerment & GBV Prevention

We work to equip young people with knowledge, confidence and tools that contribute to healthier relationships and safer communities.

  • Youth development
  • GBV prevention
  • Healthy relationships
  • Community resilience
04

Food Security & Humanitarian Support

We provide practical relief and humanitarian assistance to households experiencing food insecurity and hardship.

  • Food parcels
  • Emergency support
  • Household assistance
  • Humanitarian relief
